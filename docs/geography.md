# Pastel City geography (World v2)

The full description of the map, so you can point at any part of it and ask for a change
("fix the roads in Maple Hills", "move the marina", "more trees on Route 8"). Everything is
generated by `src/city.js` from the seed `20260925` (`Game.start` in `src/game.js`).

**Keeping it in sync:** every number in this document comes from the real build. The
**Generated facts** part at the end is the verbatim output of

```sh
node tools/geo-report.js            # the markdown below "Generated facts"
node tools/geo-report.js --check    # counts, build time per phase, sanity checks
```

Rerun it after changing `city.js` and replace that part. The hand-written sections above it
quote the same numbers. When the layout changes, update them too.

Coordinates are **tiles** (16 px) with x to the east and y to the south, from (0, 0) at the
north-west corner. `#demo&goto=<name>` jumps the camera to any district, neighbourhood,
landmark, street, lake or island. Matching ignores case, spaces and punctuation, e.g.
`tools/shot.sh out.png 'demo&goto=union station&time=2'`.

## 1. Overview

- **World:** 768 x 768 tiles (12,288 px square). A mainland of about 78% land in the
  **Pastel Sea**. The coast is a noisy rounded box, 6-62 tiles in from the world edge. District
  rectangles are always kept on land, with a noisy 5-14 tile margin.
- **Pastel Bay** is a sea inlet from the north coast between Major City and North Farms/Side
  City (x 366-533, y 0-298). Its shores are straight where the **Pastel Gate Bridge** crosses it
  (x 366 and x 500, around y 243). Its southern tip stops just short of Ironworks.
- **Islands (5):** Gull Island in the bay (sand and palms), plus Pine Island (NW corner),
  Seal Rocks (NE, rocky with boulders), Pelican Isle (SW) and Coral Key (SE) in the sea.
- **Water inland:** Mirror Lake (NW forest), Willow Lake (Clover Meadows), Heron Lake
  (the source of the **Lilac River**, which flows south between Main City and the SE farms to the
  sea) and Oasis Pool in the desert.
- **Terrain:** forests (`forest` ground with pines and round trees) cluster in the
  north-west and north-east. Meadows and grass fill the centre. The **Amber Desert** (desert,
  dunes, rock, cacti, boulders) covers the south-east. Beaches ring the sea coast, with rocky
  capes in the NW and NE. River banks are sand and lake banks are grass.
- **Layout:** as in `refs/city.png`. Three cities (Major NW, Side E, Main SW), each a 3x3
  downtown core ringed by suburbs, with their four corner blocks left wild so the rings look
  round. Ironworks sits in the middle, the farms are N, W and SE, the airport is on the SW coast,
  and nature fills the gaps.

See the ASCII map in the generated facts.

## 2. Road network and profiles

Every road is a **9-tile band** (`across` 0..8). Grids are made of *segments* (one block side,
20 tiles in cities and Ironworks, 31 in farms) and 9x9 intersection *boxes*. Merging blocks
removes the segments between them. `City.roadAt(x, y)` is the single source of truth: it
returns the band (`v`/`h`/`int`/`rail`), its profile, `across`/`along`, the street id, and
`xing` where the railway crosses.

| Profile | Where | Layout across the band |
|---|---|---|
| `avenue` | any segment touching a downtown block | 0-1 parking bays (`ave` frame 1, separators every 4 tiles, one parked-car spot per bay) · 2-3 lanes · 4 double yellow · 5-6 lanes · 7-8 bays. Zebras at both ends, a stop line on the approach lanes, traffic lights at every avenue intersection with 3+ arms (4 poles). Parking meter per bay, P sign, no-parking sign, a ticket machine, lamps, hydrants and bins on the curb. |
| `street` | suburb segments | 0-1 and 7-8 grass **verge** with street trees · 2-6 lighter asphalt with a dashed centre line. The corner squares of the boxes are verge too. Stop signs at 3+ arm junctions, 2-tile sidewalks, lamps, curb parking at the asphalt edge. |
| `rough` | Ironworks | 0 and 8 gravel · 1-7 asphalt mixed with cracked/patched/pothole/oil `rough` tiles · faded centre line. 2-tile sidewalks, sparse lamps. |
| `dirt` | farms | 0-1 and 7-8 meadow · 2-6 `dirtroad` with tyre ruts, `dirtroad_x` at junctions. No sidewalks, no lamps. |
| `highway` | between districts | 0 and 8 shoulders with edge lines · 1-3 and 5-7 carriageways (dashed divider in tiles 2 and 6) · 4 double yellow median. Lamps every 16 tiles on the verges, a speed sign at the start. `ggbridge` railings on the Pastel Gate Bridge, `bridge` tiles elsewhere. |
| `court` | cul-de-sacs | 4-wide stem plus a turning circle (the `culdesac` paint op), inside 2x2 suburb super-blocks. |

Intersections take the biggest profile that meets there (avenue > highway > rough > street > dirt).
Highways join the grids at existing boxes. The grids are aligned so that most connections are
straight: Ironworks' vertical lines continue Main City's (offset 2) and Major City's line 5.
Route 1 runs along Major's line 6 and Side's line 1.

## 3. Districts

Grid origins, sizes and block counts are in the generated facts. What each area feels like:

### Major City (city, NW)
The tallest, densest core. **Pastel Heights** (core row 2) has the Pastel Mall on a double block
plus towers and offices. **Parkside** (core rows 3-4) has **Central Park** (2x2 blocks) and the
**Police Station**, and the spawn is on the park's south sidewalk on Vanilla Avenue. The suburb
ring: **Lilac Hill** (N), **Bayview** (E, home of **Pastel Stadium** on a 2x2 block facing the
bay: a Beira-Rio style bowl on a wide paved esplanade with trees, benches and lamps, car parks
north and south, and a waterfront promenade on the bay shore across the avenue), **Maple Hills**
(S, where Route 1 and Route 8 leave; its corner-shop strip right across the road from the spawn
holds **Pastel Arms**, the nearest gun store), **Rosewood** (W). The suburbs mix
houses (four lots per block, driveways, pools, picket fences), cul-de-sac super-blocks, small
parks and corner shops. Vehicle mix: downtown on the avenues, suburbs on the streets.

### Side City (city, E)
**Coral Centre** (core row 2) and **Broadcast Square** (core rows 3-4, with the **Seabreeze Ammo**
gun store opening a downtown shopping row, and the PCTV campus on a 2x1 block: the glass tower with a helipad, three dishes and the TV mast on its roof, and a yard of outside-broadcast vans). The ring: **Sunhill** (N, Route 9 to
North Farms), **Seabreeze** (E), **Marina Heights** (S, Route 7 to the SE farms),
**Bridgeview** (W, where the Pastel Gate Bridge and Route 4 arrive). East of the grid, **Shell
Beach** runs down the coast: a palm promenade, a boardwalk, about 14 tiles of sand with
umbrellas, towels and two lifeguard huts, and buoys offshore. **Marina Drive** leads from the
east edge to **Sunrise Marina** in a cove to the south-east: a clubhouse and a car park on the
shore at the end of the drive, three piers 11 tiles apart (x 738, 749, 760) with yachts, sailboats
and motorboats moored along both sides at full scale (only where the whole hull is on water),
and a breakwater quay along the cove's north side.

### Main City (city, SW)
**Union Square** (core row 2), with **Union Station** on a 2x3 block that reaches north into
**Railside**. The railway comes down the block's middle, with platforms on both sides, the
station hall on the west platform, a taxi rank and two car parks. **Mercy Hill** (core rows 3-4)
has **Mercy Hospital** on a 2x1 block with an ambulance bay, a helipad and a car park. The ring:
**Railside** (N), **Riverbend** (E, Route 6 to the SE farms over the Lilac River; its corner shops
by Mercy Hill hold **Union Firearms**), **Portside** (S), **Westgate** (W, the Airport Expressway).
South of the grid is the **Port**: a container yard and warehouses (y 696-710), the main quay
(y 711-715) with three gantry cranes whose booms reach over the container ship (11 x 60 tiles)
moored alongside, a 15-tile harbour basin (y 716-730), and a mole (y 731-738) with bollards and
more containers. It belongs to Main City as its own neighbourhood.

### Ironworks (industrial, centre)
6x5 grid of mostly 2x1 long blocks on rough roads. **Freight Yards** (W) holds the freight yard
(2x3 blocks: the railway's north end, two sidings with parked wagons, container stacks).
**Smokestack Row** (centre) has factories with chimneys, warehouses and depots; **Anvil Surplus**,
an army-surplus gun store, stands at a warehouse gate on Route 4's line. **Dockside
Works** (E, by the bay's south tip) has the **Boost Garage** car park, container yards and the
tank farm. Vehicle mix: trucks, semis, tankers, flatbeds, mixers.

### North Farms, West Farms, South-East Farms (rural)
3x3 grids of 31x31 lots on dirt roads. Each has six field lots (four crop quadrants split by
dirt tracks, one quadrant a farmstead with house, barn, silos and haystack), one orchard, one
fenced pasture with a barn and cows, and one **farm town** (gas station, shop, bar, diner, which
holds the district payphone). Neighbourhoods: **Honeyfield** / **Clover Hollow** (N),
**Wheatmere** / **Old Mill** (W), **Sundown Acres** / **Orchard Glen** (SE).

### Pastel Airport (x 30-176, y 495-725)
Sized for full-scale planes (airliner 17 x 16 tiles, prop plane 7 x 9). **Airfield**: a 17x200
north-south runway (x 38-54) with shoulders, thresholds and a centre dash, an airliner lined up
on it, an 11-wide parallel taxiway (x 62-72) far enough east that wingtips on the two never meet,
three 11-tile rapid-exit links, and an apron (x 73-121) with an 11-wide taxilane, three airliner
gates 20 tiles apart (nose to the terminal), a remote stand, two prop planes, three ground
helipads and two 16x12 hangars. **Terminal**: the 10x62 terminal, a control tower (`ctower`,
h 220, blue light), a drop-off plaza with a taxi rank, and a large car park. Airport Road (from West
Farms) and the Airport Expressway (from Main City) meet at its junction.

### Wild districts
**Pinewood Wilds** (Pinewood Forest, Mirror Lake, Fernwood, North Shore) · **Cliffside** (NE
woods and rocky cape) · **Clover Meadows** (meadows and Willow Lake between West Farms,
Ironworks and Main City) · **Lilac Valley** (Heron Lake, Lilac River, Lilac Delta) · **Amber
Desert** (Sagebrush Flats, Cactus Flats, Amber Dunes, Oasis Springs with Oasis Pool) · **Pastel
Bay** (the bay water, Gull Island, and the Pastel Gate Bridge as its own neighbourhood) ·
**Pastel Sea** (open sea and four islands).

How naming works: neighbourhood rects are painted into a per-tile map in order, cities last.
Land-only rects skip open sea, and the bay and sea only paint water. Any tile left over takes
the nearest neighbourhood (breadth-first fill), so every tile has an address. Within a district
the rects don't overlap; the checks in `geo-report.js` enforce this. `City.placeAt(x, y)` reads
that map. Sidewalks and verges within 3 tiles of a road carry the road's name.

## 5. Railway

A single vertical line (`c.rail`, rails on tiles x 284-285, ballast 283 and 286). It runs from the Ironworks freight
yard buffer (y 373) south through the Ironworks boundary road, a fenced cutting through the gap,
and Main City's north avenue (two level crossings) to the Union Station buffer (y 566). The
train stops at the platforms (y 540, 8 s dwell) and waits at both ends. Buffers face the track
at both ends. The two sidings in the freight yard are scenery, with wagons and their own buffers.

## 7. Anchors

- **Spawn:** Central Park's south sidewalk (Parkside, Vanilla Avenue). The **starter car** (a
  sport) waits in the parking bay right below, and the **tank** is on the sidewalk 100 px east,
  with props and lamps cleared around it.
- **Boost garage:** the empty car park in Dockside Works, by the bay.
- **Gun stores (5):** `c.gunshops` = `{ id, name, area, x, y, ang, b }`. The door mat (`props:gunshop_door`,
  a floor sprite) is on the sidewalk right in front of the door, cleared of props and cars.
  0 Pastel Arms (Major City, 11 tiles from the spawn), 1 Seabreeze Ammo (Side City), 2 Union
  Firearms (Main City), 3 Anvil Surplus (Ironworks), 4 Dusty Barrel (a roadside store on Route 6's
  south verge by the SE Farms). `goto=Gun Store 1`..`5` or `GUN STORE: <AREA>` stops just outside
  the mat; `demo&shop=i` opens store i.
- **Payphones (8):** one in each city's downtown, one in Ironworks, one in each farm town and
  one at the airport plaza.
- **Parked cars:** every curb bay, stall, driveway and yard is a spot (`parkSpots`, `stalls`,
  `parked`). At start, every car within 1400 px of the spawn is kept, plus police and ambulance
  spots, plus a random share of the rest, up to about 380 cars in total. Spots without their own
  `models` use `City.regionAt(x, y).models` (the table in the generated facts).
- **Helicopters:** parked on the roof helipads of the police station, the hospital, the PCTV
  tower and about half of the helipad skyscrapers (spawned landed on the roof, `alt` = building
  height, not boardable on foot). Boardable ones stand on ground helipads: three at the airport
  (`goto=Heliport` is the one near the hangars), one in the hospital car park (`goto=Hospital Helipad`)
  and one in the corner of the stadium car park. Parked cars keep 60 px clear of the ground pads. No car, stall, curb, crate or mission spot sits on water or in a wall (a `geo-report --check` check).
  The full list is in the generated facts.
- **Regenerate:** `City.build(seed)`. Everything is seeded from the seed through `rng()` and a
  seeded 256x256 noise lattice (no `Math.random`).

## 8. Known issues and ideas

- Neighbourhood rects are band partitions, so a neighbourhood like Mirror Lake covers a whole
  east-west band of the forest, not just the lake.
- `quay` only has an edge stripe along the top, so the main quay's edge row (water to the south)
  uses the same striped tile as the mole's (water to the north). There's no `bridge_v` yet (no vertical road
  bridge needed so far), and no side-platform edge tiles. (The runway has both edge lines since
  2026-09-26: `tiles:runway` frame 4 is the east edge.)
- The forced land around the airport makes the south-west coast straighter than elsewhere.
- Ideas: a lighthouse on Gull Island, a coastal road along the south, bus stops on avenues, a
  ferry across the bay, bigger forests with trails, farm tracks out to the fields' far corners.
