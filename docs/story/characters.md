# Characters

Status: **v1 draft** 2026-09-27. Owner: screenplay-agent. The user's names from `mobs.txt` are canon:
Big Tony, Dimitri, Han, Katherine ("Russian love interest"), Francesca ("Italian love interest"),
Lee ("Chinese love interest"), Commissaire Dick ("chief of police"). Surnames, lieutenants and
personalities below are proposals.

Each entry: **who**, **wants from the player**, **how you meet**, **mission flavour**, **a line**.
Speech lines are written for the bitmap font (short, capitals in game).

## The Moretti Family (Italian, Side City)

### Big Tony Moretti, the boss
- **Who.** Sixty, huge, loud, cries at weddings and at his own jokes. Built the family on "basil" and
  loyalty, and is quietly terrified that nobody will be able to run it after him.
- **Wants.** Someone reliable who isn't family, so nobody at the table can call it nepotism. Then, a
  soldier for the war with the Orlovs.
- **Meet.** Already in your phone as `BIG TONY` (boost jobs). His first real call comes after two jobs for
  Mama Rosa: "My mother says you don't talk much. I like that in a person."
- **Missions.** Car jobs (he loves muscle cars), hitting Orlov sedans and shipments, and big set pieces
  on the Pastel Gate Bridge.
- **Line.** "IN THIS FAMILY WE FORGIVE. AFTER."

### Mama Rosa, Tony's mother
- **Who.** Runs Rosa's Trattoria in Side City and, from the kitchen, the family. Nobody has seen her sit down.
- **Wants.** Things delivered hot and on time, and a grandchild's future (Francesca's) sorted out.
- **Meet.** Already in your phone as `MAMA ROSA` ("Package work"). The first Moretti job is hers.
- **Missions.** Deliveries against the clock ("it gets cold").
- **Line.** "YOU LOOK HUNGRY. DRIVE FASTER, THEN EAT."

### Sal "the Captain" Bruno
- **Who.** Runs the family's boats at Sunrise Marina. A captain's hat, seasick on the pier.
- **Wants.** Shipments picked up, Russian boats sunk (when boats are driveable), a quiet night.
- **Meet.** After Moretti respect reaches Friendly.
- **Missions.** Night pickups at the marina and Shell Beach, escorts along the coast.
- **Line.** "SHE'S A BEAUTIFUL BOAT. DON'T SCRATCH HER."

### Francesca Moretti (love interest)
- **Who.** Tony's daughter, thirty, the family's bookkeeper. Smarter than everyone at the table, and she
  knows it. Runs the numbers for the casinos and knows exactly how much the war is costing.
- **Her stake.** She wants to **run the family**. Tony loves her and won't hear of it ("a boss needs
  to be feared"). The Orlov war is burning the money she built. If she takes over, she ends the war on her
  terms; if she fails, she's married off to a business partner of Tony's in the country.
- **Wants from the player.** An ally who reports to her, not to her father. Proof that the war can be
  won, or ended, without Tony's methods.
- **Meet.** At the trattoria, when a Moretti job goes wrong and she's the one who fixes it with a phone
  call and a spreadsheet.
- **Missions.** Quiet sabotage: steal the Orlov ledger, move money before Tony notices, make deals behind
  his back. Her missions can raise Moretti respect while hiding things from Tony.
- **Line.** "PAPA SOLVES PROBLEMS WITH A SHOTGUN. I SOLVE THEM WITH INTEREST."

## The Orlov Syndicate (Russian, Major City and Ironworks)

### Dimitri Orlov, the boss
- **Who.** Former chess champion, fifty, immaculate black suit, eats the same lunch every day. Obsessed
  with time: he tells you how many seconds late you are. Terrifying because he's never in a hurry.
- **Wants.** A precise instrument. He tests the player like a new piece on the board before he trusts
  them with the war.
- **Meet.** A black sedan pulls up next to the player, the window rolls down, and a phone is handed out.
  It rings. "You have forty seconds to decide."
- **Missions.** Timed deliveries, precise hits, jobs with a schedule. His briefings give exact times.
- **Line.** "YOU ARE FOUR SECONDS LATE. I WILL REMEMBER EACH ONE."

### Yuri "Crane" Volkov
- **Who.** Runs the Syndicate's Ironworks: the freight yard, the Boost Garage, the port when it moves
  to the bay. Gentle giant, names his gantry cranes, hates the Morettis' red cars "because they are loud".
- **Wants.** Cars stripped, containers moved, the rail line kept running.
- **Meet.** The first Orlov job after Dimitri's test.
- **Missions.** Boosts to the garage, stealing Moretti muscle cars, the train (later), containers.
- **Line.** "THIS CRANE IS CALLED NATASHA. SHE HAS NEVER DROPPED A BOX. DON'T BE THE FIRST."

### The Professor (Lev Sokolov)
- **Who.** A dean at the Major City university (world-v3 W6) who launders the Syndicate's money through
  research grants. Tweed, footnotes, zero morals.
- **Wants.** "Donors" persuaded, a rival academic's car relocated to the bay.
- **Meet.** After the university is built, at Orlov respect Friendly.
- **Missions.** Campus jobs, intimidation, collections.
- **Line.** "PEER REVIEW IS VERY HARSH THIS YEAR."

### Katherine (love interest)
- **Who.** Dimitri's half-sister, the Syndicate's lawyer. Charming, funny, always three moves ahead,
  and the only person who beats Dimitri at chess (she lets him win on his birthday).
- **Her stake.** She wants **out**, with enough money to never come back. She's building a case file on
  the Syndicate as insurance, and a way off the mainland. If Dimitri finds the file, she's finished; if
  she leaves too early, Commissaire Dick (who suspects the file exists) gets it.
- **Wants from the player.** A driver and an accomplice she can trust more than her brother. She may
  also use the player as a decoy, and she'll say so afterwards, with an apology and a bonus.
- **Meet.** At the police station: she's the lawyer who walks out of it with an Orlov soldier the player
  just helped get arrested (a story beat after an Orlov job).
- **Missions.** Getaways, stealing documents, planting evidence on the Morettis (or on Dick), a final
  escape by boat, train or plane (the airport).
- **Line.** "I DON'T BREAK THE LAW. I READ IT VERY CAREFULLY."

## The Orchid Society (Chinese, Main City)

### Han, the boss
- **Who.** Seventy, soft-spoken, owns half of Main City's buildings through companies with boring
  names. Plays golf every Sunday with Commissaire Dick and lets him win. Never raises his voice.
- **Wants.** Stability. The Italian-Russian war is bad for business (and good for arms sales). He wants
  the player as a "consultant" who does what his own people can't be seen doing.
- **Meet.** A purple sports car picks the player up. Lee drives; Han sits in the back and offers tea
  from a thermos.
- **Missions.** Clean jobs that "don't warn the cops": removing evidence, "misunderstandings",
  protecting the Commissaire's reputation, quietly playing the Morettis and the Orlovs against each other.
- **Line.** "THERE ARE NO PROBLEMS. ONLY INVOICES."

### Madame Mei
- **Who.** Runs the Orchid Club, the Society's hostess club and hotel (off-screen). Knows every secret
  in Pastel City because every secret has been told in her bar. Sharp, businesslike, protective of her staff.
- **Wants.** Her people safe, customers who pay, and certain customers who don't pay "reminded".
- **Meet.** At Orchid respect Friendly, through Lee.
- **Missions.** Collections, protecting her staff from bad customers, blackmail material recovered.
- **Line.** "EVERYONE TALKS HERE. THAT'S THE PRODUCT."

### Lee (love interest)
- **Who.** The Society's best driver: street racer, getaway driver, Han's chauffeur. Fearless behind
  the wheel, allergic to being told what to do. (Gender: the user's call; the draft uses "she".)
- **Her stake.** Commissaire Dick holds her younger brother in the Main City lock-up on a charge he
  "might drop", which keeps Lee driving for the Society and the Society paying Dick. She wants her
  brother out and the Society **free of Dick**, even if Han thinks the alliance is worth it.
- **Wants from the player.** Someone who can drive, and who isn't afraid of the police because they
  don't know any better.
- **Meet.** She challenges the player to a checkpoint race through Main City (the first Orchid job).
- **Missions.** Races, getaways, tailing Dick's men, and eventually a jailbreak.
- **Line.** "BRAKES ARE A SUGGESTION."

## The police

### Commissaire Dick, chief of police
- **Who.** Chief of the Pastel City police, on Han's payroll, running for mayor on a "tough on crime"
  campaign funded by crime. Vain (his face is on every campaign poster), cowardly, and far from stupid.
- **Wants.** Votes, money, and a big arrest before the election: a Moretti or an Orlov, he doesn't care
  which. He'll use the player as an informant if he catches them.
- **Meet.** On a campaign poster first. In person when the player is busted (after the wanted level
  exists), or in an Orchid job that protects his reputation.
- **Missions.** Jobs "for the city": framing a mob, recovering his lost police cruiser before the press
  finds out, escorting his campaign bus. Late game: the one person all three mobs might agree to remove.
- **Line.** "I'M NOT CORRUPT. I'M PRIVATELY FUNDED."
