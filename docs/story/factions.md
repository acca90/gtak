Status: **v1 draft** 2026-09-27, for the user to react to. Owner: screenplay-agent.
Source: the user's `mobs.txt` (quoted as written in each section). Everything under **Expansion** is
screenplay-agent's proposal and can be changed; the quoted lines are canon.

and jokes; culture flavours names, food, business and style, and never becomes the joke itself.

## 1. The Italian mob: the Moretti Family

**The user's lines (`mobs.txt`):**
> - In war against Russian mob
> - Making points with the Italins lower your respect with the Russians
> - Leader: Big Tony
> - Involved in smugle pot
> - Italian mob drivers red muscle cars
> - Italian member have black hair and red t shirt.
> - Based on Side City

**Expansion.**

- **Identity and style.** Loud, sentimental, generous and petty. They throw parties, hold grudges for
  decades and pay on time. Members wear the red T-shirt like a football jersey; the cars are red
  muscle cars (the `BRUISER` model, hence "the Bruisers"), driven fast and parked badly.
- **Leader: Big Tony Moretti.** Big voice, big heart, big temper. Also the existing phone contact
  `BIG TONY` ("Wheels for clients"), so the player already knows him. See `characters.md`.
- **Lieutenants and contacts.**
  - **Mama Rosa**, Tony's mother, runs *Rosa's Trattoria*, the family's real HQ. The existing phone
    contact `MAMA ROSA` ("Package work") becomes hers. She gives the first jobs.
  - **Sal "the Captain" Bruno**, runs the boats at Sunrise Marina. He has never been to sea further than
    Gull Island and calls every boat "she".
  - **Francesca Moretti**, Tony's daughter, keeps the family's books (love interest, see characters).
- **Business in the world.** Pot comes in by boat to **Sunrise Marina** at night and goes out through
  Side City's fun businesses (beach bars, the casinos and the stadium after world-v3 W7). In the game:
  marina and beach drop-offs, night deliveries, boats (later), and the red muscle cars in traffic.
- **Turf.** **Side City** (all of it: Coral Centre, Broadcast Square, Sunhill, Seabreeze, Marina
  Heights, Bridgeview), Shell Beach and Sunrise Marina. With world-v3, Side City becomes the "fun city",
  which suits them. The **Pastel Gate Bridge** is the front line with the Russians (it lands in
  Bridgeview on one side and Major City on the other). They also like the **South-East Farms** (Route 7):
  fields where "basil" grows (proposal, see Questions).
- **Relationships.** At war with the Orlovs, who they call "the Undertakers". Polite with the Orchid
  Society, which they see as bankers with bad taste in cops.

## 2. The Russian mob: the Orlov Syndicate

**The user's lines (`mobs.txt`):**
> - In war against Italian mob
> - Making points with the Russian lower your respect with the Italians
> - Leader: Dimitri
> - Involved in smugle cocaine
> - Russian mobs drive black sedans
> - They are blonde and wear black clothes
> - Based in Major City and present in Ironworks

**Expansion.**

- **Identity and style.** Cold, tidy, punctual, and much funnier than they think they are. Everything is
  a schedule. Black clothes, blonde hair, black sedans (`LUMEN SEDAN` in black) driven at exactly the speed
  limit until they aren't. The Italians call them "the Undertakers", and the Orlovs take it as a compliment.
- **Leader: Dimitri Orlov.** Former chess champion, obsessed with time and order. See `characters.md`.
- **Lieutenants and contacts.**
  - **Yuri "Crane" Volkov**, runs the Syndicate's side of **Ironworks**: the freight yard today, and the
    port when world-v3 W3 moves it to the bay. Talks to his gantry cranes.
  - **The Professor** (Lev Sokolov), a dean at the future **Major City university** (world-v3 W6) who
    launders the money through research grants nobody has ever read.
  - **Katherine**, Dimitri's half-sister and the Syndicate's lawyer (love interest, see characters).
- **Business in the world.** Cocaine comes in inside shipping containers at the port (Ironworks after
  W3), moves by rail and truck through the Ironworks yards, and is sold in Major City's towers, offices
  and campus. In the game: container yards, the freight yard, the train, trucks, and black sedans.
- **Turf.** **Major City** (Pastel Heights, Parkside, Lilac Hill, Bayview, Maple Hills, Rosewood) and
  **Ironworks** (Freight Yards, Smokestack Row, Dockside Works). Ironworks is their "presence", not their
  home: fewer members, but the business runs through it. The Boost Garage in Dockside Works is theirs.
- **Relationships.** At war with the Morettis ("the tomato people", Dimitri says, without smiling).
  Cautious with the Orchid Society, because the Orchids own the police and Dimitri doesn't own anything
  he can't count.

## 3. The Chinese mob: the Orchid Society

**The user's lines (`mobs.txt`):**
> - Allied with corrupt cops
> - Chinese missions do not warn the cops.
> - Leader: Han
> - Involved in prostitution
> - They drive purple sports car
> - They have black hair and purple t shirt.
> - Based in Main City.

**Expansion.**

- **Identity and style.** Quiet money. They don't need to shout: they own the buildings, the paperwork
  and the police. Purple T-shirts, purple sports cars (`VELOCE GT` in purple), the best drivers in the city.
  Their humour is dry: everything is "a misunderstanding" that can be "cleared up".
- **Leader: Han.** Patient, polite, a landlord to half of Main City and golf partner of Commissaire Dick.
  See `characters.md`.
- **Lieutenants and contacts.**
  - **Madame Mei**, manager of the *Orchid Club*, a members-only hostess club and hotel. It is the
    Society's business and its gossip exchange; it stays off-screen (a facade and a door, nothing inside).
  - **Lee**, the Society's best driver (love interest, see characters).
  - **Commissaire Dick** is not a member, but he is on the payroll (see Relationships).
- **Business in the world.** The Orchid Club and the hotels (off-screen), plus real estate, parking
  lots and "security". With world-v3 W7, Side City gets brothels: see Questions (they're on Italian turf).
  The brothel of backlog 6 removes 2 wanted stars, which fits perfectly if it's an Orchid business:
  "the cops who drink there don't remember your face".
- **Turf.** **Main City** (Union Square, Mercy Hill, Railside, Riverbend, Portside, Westgate), the
  pier after W3, and the airport road. World-v3 W5 puts the **police HQ and the town hall** in Main City,
  right on their turf, which is why the alliance works.
- **Relationships.** **Allied with the corrupt cops**: Commissaire Dick takes Han's money. Neutral in the
  Italian-Russian war and happy to sell to both sides. Their jobs **don't warn the cops** (the rule is a
  contract with the police code, see `mechanics.md` §6).

## 4. The police

- **Commissaire Dick**, chief of police (the user's line: "Commissaire Dick, chief of police").
  Corrupt, vain, and running for mayor. See `characters.md`.
- The police are not a faction with respect in v1: they react to crimes (cops-v1 today, the wanted level
  of backlog 2 later). The Orchid alliance changes when they look away (`mechanics.md` §6).

## 5. Freelancers (no mob)

The existing phone contacts that don't belong to a mob stay as neutral odd-jobs, so there is always
work that doesn't touch respect:

- **Zed** ("Fireworks", torch jobs): a pyromaniac with a sense of theatre.
- **Lucky Lou** ("Street races", rush jobs): a bookie who bets on everything, including you.
